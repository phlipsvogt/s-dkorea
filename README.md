# Yeojeong (여정) — South Korea Travel Atlas

An interactive travel atlas featuring 220 curated destinations across South Korea, anchored from Yongil Elementary School & Inha University in Michuhol-gu, Incheon.

## Features

- **220 Curated Spots:** Detailed travel times, subway/bus connections, insider tips, budget tiers, and Korean Hangul names.
- **Regions Covered:** Inha & Michuhol-gu, Songdo, Incheon Open Port & Islands (Yeongjongdo, Muuido, Ganghwado), Suwon & Gyeonggi-do, Seoul, Gangwon-do & East Coast, Jeolla & Chungcheong, Gyeongju, Busan, and Jeju Island.
- **Interactive Map:** Powered by Leaflet & Mapbox Retina HD (@2x vector tiles) with category color coding, route polyline tracking, and live filtering.
- **Direct Navigation:** 1-click deep links to Naver Map, KakaoMap, and Google Maps Photos.
- **Views:** Split-View (Atlas + Map), Full Interactive Map, Compact Table, and 4K Video Lounge.

## Quick Start

Open `index.html` in any modern web browser.
